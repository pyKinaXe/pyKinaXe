Translation test
================

Shifts one frame along x and along y over the range in config_translation.yaml
and records whether the reference spots and the spot grid are still found.
Steps that would push the content out of the frame are reported as not
testable.

Run (from the repository root):

    python validation_paper/validate_robustness_of_image_analysis/translation/translation_sweep.py

--no-panels gives a terminal-only run, --help lists all options.

Output: one marked figure per offset in output/; the detected range is printed
to the terminal.
