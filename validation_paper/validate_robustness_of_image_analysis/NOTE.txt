Robustness of the image analysis against rotation and translation
==================================================================

Rotates or shifts one frame of the rat dataset step by step and records
whether pyKinaXe still finds the reference spots and the spot grid.

Run (from the repository root):

    python validation_paper/validate_robustness_of_image_analysis/rotation/rotation_sweep.py
    python validation_paper/validate_robustness_of_image_analysis/translation/translation_sweep.py

--no-panels gives a terminal-only run, --help lists all options. Range, frame
and data source are set in rotation/config_rotation.yaml and
translation/config_translation.yaml.

Output: one marked figure per step in rotation/output/ and
translation/output/ (excluded from git); the results are printed to the
terminal. A panel without markings is a failed detection.
