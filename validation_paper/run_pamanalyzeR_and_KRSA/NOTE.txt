pamgeneAnalyzeR and KRSA on the raw PamChip images, timed
=========================================================

Measures the runtime of the R tools pamgeneAnalyzeR (image analysis) and KRSA
(kinase analysis) on the data pyKinaXe reads.

Run (from this folder; missing R packages are installed):

    cd validation_paper/run_pamanalyzeR_and_KRSA
    Rscript run_pamanalyzeR_and_KRSA.R --unmodified --datasets benchmarking --output-dir output_unmodified
    Rscript run_krsa_on_bionavigator.R --output-dir output_krsa_on_bn
    Rscript run_krsa_on_bionavigator.R --krsa-scope across --output-dir output_krsa_on_bn_across

Optional, pamgeneAnalyzeR chained into KRSA:

    Rscript run_pamanalyzeR_and_KRSA.R

--help on either script lists all options.

Output: timing_summary.txt and timing.csv in the output folder (excluded from
git). The curated runs are kept in results/. run_krsa_on_bionavigator.R
without --output-dir overwrites results/krsa_on_bionavigator/.
